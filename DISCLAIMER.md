# Disclaimer

This software is a research and education tool. It is not financial advice,
and nobody involved in writing it is a licensed financial adviser.

It defaults to a paper trading account, which uses fake money. If you connect
a live account and act on what this produces, you are making your own
investment decisions and carrying your own losses.

The data comes from public filings and free price feeds. All of it is lagged,
some of it is wrong, and the app tries to tell you which is which. It does not
always succeed.

Past performance of any portfolio shown here, including the ones on the
leaderboard, does not predict future performance. The leaderboard exists
specifically to show how often well known investors fail to beat a plain index
fund.

The eight point return target in the config is an aim, not a forecast, and
nothing in this software can make it happen.
